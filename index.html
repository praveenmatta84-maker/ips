Simulator Documentation

1. Environment Overview

This is an advanced 3D real-time simulation of a 12m × 6m indoor environment. It features furnished architecture (desks, sofas, a wall-mounted TV, and dual rotating ceiling fans) combined with a robust Hybrid Sensor Fusion tracking pipeline.

2. Tracking Architecture

  • BLE Beacons (A, B, C, D): Simulates Bluetooth Low Energy RSSI trilateration with configurable noise ($\sigma$) and a 1D Kalman filter to smooth signal fluctuations.
  • ArUco Vision Camera: Simulates corner-mounted CCTV cameras tracking visual markers for optical position estimation.
  • Sensor Fusion: Dynamically weights camera and BLE positioning outputs via a customizable blending slider.

3. Navigation & Controls

  • Mouse Look: Double-click inside the 3D viewport to capture your cursor and look around smoothly in First-Person View (FPV). Press ESC or double-click again to release the cursor.
  • Movement: Use W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys to walk through the environment. Collision detection prevents walking through walls and furniture.
  • Dashboard Panels: Use the top-left control panel to toggle BLE beacons, vision cameras, and Kalman filters, or adjust noise and sensor weight sliders. You can also upload a custom face texture for the avatar.
